✦ Geometric-Mean Softmax Consensus Formulation

The deliberation reaches the pupil not through majority dictation, but through a balanced, shared geometric-mean consensus across the council:

$$P_{\text{consensus}}(v) = \frac{\prod_{k=1}^K \left(P_k(v)\right)^{w_k}}{\sum_{v'} \prod_{k=1}^K \left(P_k(v')\right)^{w_k}} = \text{Softmax}\left(\sum_{k=1}^K w_k \cdot z_k(v)\right)$$
With multi-faculty KL loss $\mathcal{L}_{\text{multi-faculty}} = \sum_{k} \beta_k D_{\text{KL}}(P_{\text{consensus}} \parallel P_{\text{pupil}})$ and $\beta$-ramp epoch scheduling.
